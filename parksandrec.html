Municipal field guide / Issue 07

Parks and Recreation

In Pawnee, public parks are for everyone: neighbors, wildlife, future generations, and the occasional very enthusiastic town meeting. This field guide celebrates the people who look after shared green space and the small choices that help it thrive.

The NBC comedy, created by Greg Daniels and Michael Schur, ran from 2009 to 2015 for seven seasons and 125 episodes. A reunion special followed in 2020.

Field notes / Leave the park better

A greener day in Pawnee

Action list / 04
  1. 01
    Grow for this place

    Choose native plants suited to your area to give local pollinators food and shelter.

  2. 02
    Leave no trace

    Pack out litter, stay on marked paths, and leave plants and wildlife where they are.

  3. 03
    Use things again

    Bring a refillable bottle, borrow tools, and reuse supplies before buying new ones.

  4. 04
    Make room for everyone

    Keep shared spaces welcoming, accessible, and cared for by the whole community.
