Character study // Greendale, Colorado

Community

At Greendale Community College, a study group becomes an excuse to turn sitcom rules inside out. Created by Dan Harmon, the series ran for six seasons and 110 episodes, moving from NBC to Yahoo Screen and repeatedly making its own genre, cast, and audience part of the joke.
