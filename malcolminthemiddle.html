A family sitcom with the volume up

Malcolm
in the Middle

Created by Linwood Boomer, this Fox original follows a gifted kid trying to survive life in the middle of a loud, unpredictable family. It ran from January 9, 2000, to May 14, 2006, across seven seasons and 151 episodes.

Fun Facts

  1. It was the first Fox series to win a Peabody Award.
  2. Jane Kaczmarek earned an Emmy nomination for every season she played Lois.
  3. Frankie Muniz was nominated for an Emmy as lead actor in 2003.
  4. Its single-camera format was unusual for sitcoms at the time.
  5. The pilot is the only episode to reveal the family surname: Wilkerson.
