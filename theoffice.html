The Office

The Office is an American mockumentary sitcom television series. It is based on the BBC series The Office created by Ricky Gervai and Stephen Merchant, and adapted for NBC by Greg Daniels. The show depicts the everyday work lives of office employees at the Scranton, Pennsylvania branch of the fictional Dunder Mifflin Paper Company. It aired from March 24, 2005, to May 16, 2013, for a total of nine seasons consisting of 201 episodes.

Fun Facts

  1. The Office was originally a British series created by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant.
  2. Bob Odenkirk was the original choice to play Michael Scott, but Steve Carell took the role after another show of his was canceled. Rainn Wilson also auditioned for Michael before landing the role of Dwight.
  3. John Krasinski shot the opening credit footage of Scranton, Pennsylvania, while driving around researching the area.
  4. The gas station rest stop where Jim proposes to Pam was entirely built as a replica set, costing roughly $250,000.
  5. The show's theme song was composed by Jay Ferguson and performed by The Scrantones.
  6. The most skipped episode was "Scott's Tots".
